A small, playable tribute to mechanical and electromechanical amusement machines. Miniature models, moving roads, steel balls, relays and the stubborn ingenuity of an era before screens ran the show.

🚘
Kasco · 1959 · Driving

Mini Drive

Steer a miniature car over a moving mechanical roadway. Keep it on the road to build your score before the clock runs out.

BEST 00000
Cointronics · 1960s · Skill

Ball Walk

Manipulate two steel rails to coax a ball uphill toward the jackpot. Five balls per round. Opening them too far sends the ball into a scoring pocket.

BEST 0000
1940s-style tabletop · Steel ball

Shoot the Moon

A vertical wooden tabletop with two hinged steel rails. Build momentum, keep the ball supported, then deliberately drop it into a planet scoring cup.

BEST 00000
Inspired by 1979 obstacle toys

Marble Mechanica

Original four-station obstacle course inspired by Screwball Scramble. Operate a spring launcher, balance a marble at the center of a teetering bridge, survive timed jumping pads, and align a rotary gate.

BEST 00000
Inspired by the 1946 wooden classic

Tilt Labyrinth

Use two virtual tilt knobs, or drag the board, to roll a steel marble through a wooden maze. Avoid holes and hit the brass goal.

BEST 00000
🐻
Seeburg · 1949 · Rifle gallery

Shoot the Bear

A moving mechanical bear crosses a painted forest. Aim the light rifle, fire, and score bonus targets when the bear rears up. 20 shots.

BEST 00000
Inspired by 1933 traveling cranes · Prize skill

Electric Crane

Position a suspended claw over rotating prizes. Time the descent, grip a prize, and deliver it to the chute. Five mechanical drops per round.

BEST 00000
Williams · 1957/58 · Miniature bowling

Ten Strike

Aim a little manikin bowler and wind his spring release. Find the off-center strike pocket while clearing five miniature bowling frames.

BEST 00000
Original concept · Relay sorting machine

Relay Switchyard

Throw electromechanical switches to send colored parcels into matching chutes. The belt speeds up, packages bunch together, and mistakes cost fuses.

About this prototype: All artwork and obstacle layouts are original. Rail games use position-dependent gravity, inertia and contact geometry; static rod openings cannot simply cruise to the jackpot. Obstacle timing, balance, and tilt visuals are also improved. This is not a licensed recreation or laboratory-perfect simulation. No downloads, accounts or network connection are needed. The game works offline after the HTML file is saved on your device.
