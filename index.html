A small, playable tribute to mechanical and electromechanical amusement machines. Miniature models, moving roads, steel balls, relays and the stubborn ingenuity of an era before screens ran the show.

Kasco · 1959 · Driving

Mini Drive

Steer a miniature car over a moving mechanical roadway. Keep it on the road to build your score before the clock runs out.

Cointronics · 1960s · Skill

Ball Walk

Manipulate two steel rails to coax a ball uphill toward the jackpot. Five balls per round. Opening them too far sends the ball into a scoring pocket.

Shoot the Bear

A moving mechanical bear crosses a painted forest. Aim the light rifle, fire, and score bonus targets when the bear rears up. 20 shots.
